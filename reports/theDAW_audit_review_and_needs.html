◆ theDAW — Audit Review, Corrected State & Actual Needs

A 4-sonnet read-only review of theDAW_70_feature_audit_with_OSS_references.html, re-verified against the current working tree (the audit ran against danieljtrujillo/theDAW · main and predates our local work). Plus the scout-team clone of all 7 referenced OSS projects. 2026-09-15 (rev 2 — scope-corrected).

Scope rule (what counts as "the DAW"). Only the DAW's own surfaces count: EDIT/arrangement, the mixer, the piano roll, the session grid, and the DJ engine. Two adjacent subsystems live in the same repo but are not the DAW and are excluded from every verdict below: Chimera (backend/modules/chimera/, the auto-mashup renderer — its internal duck() / stretch.py are Chimera's own DSP), and dawimport (backend/modules/dawimport/ableton.py, a parser that reads .als files — parsing another DAW's warp/take/comp data on import is not theDAW implementing them). Rev 1 of this report let three verdicts lean on those; they are corrected here.

1 · The verdict: the audit badly understated theDAW

The audit reported 29 covered / 14 partial / 27 not-evidenced. Re-checked against the real tree by four independent sonnet auditors, spot-verified by me, and scoped to the DAW proper, the true state is:

44actually COVERED (was 29)
15partial / scoped (was 14)
11truly ABSENT (was 27)
70accounted for

So only 11 of 70 DAW primitives are genuinely missing. The audit listed 27 as "not evidenced" — but 13 of those already exist and 3 exist partially; its recursive scan simply didn't reach the files. Most of theDAW's real capability lives in WaveformEditor.tsx, djEngine.ts, editorStore.ts, and lib/ (editorAutosave.ts, notechart.ts, rackEffects.ts), which the audit under-scanned.
