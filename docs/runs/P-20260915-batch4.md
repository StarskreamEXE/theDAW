## T06 — what landed

- `lib/metronome.ts`: a pure rolling-lookahead scheduler (`METRONOME_TICK_MS`
  100, `METRONOME_LOOKAHEAD_SEC` 0.25) on the engine `AudioContext`. Three
  invariants, each tested: the anchor's prediction of the transport position
  never diverges from the transport's own report by more than
  `METRONOME_RESYNC_SEC` (0.05) — a seek stops queued-but-unsounded clicks and
  re-anchors; `lastBeat` only moves forward (no beat twice); a beat already
  behind `ctx.currentTime` is skipped, not fired late. Clicks are laid out
  **per bar** so a 7/8 downbeat always clicks (a global quarter-note grid
  would silence every second downbeat). Accent on beat 1 from `meterMap`.
  Click primitive reused from our own `LatencyCalibrator.tsx`.
  `setMuteTest(fn)` is the mute-policy extension point (design provenance:
  Tracktion `ClickNode` `isMutedAtTime`, named; file never opened).
- `state/metronomeStore.ts`: `enabled`, `volume`, `accent`, `countInBars`
  (0|1|2), persisted; builds the one-event tempo map from `editorStore.bpm`,
  cached by value (tempoMap caches by array identity); meter from
  `beatClock.meterMap`. Count-in schedules audio and one timer only; the
  transport is released from that timer, so the playhead never advances and
  nothing records during the count. A stale deferred release (tab changed
  mid-count) is dropped, not fired into DJ/VJ. Click gated to the EDIT
  timeline entry (a library `<audio>` play would otherwise click on one beat
  forever).
- `PlayerFooter.tsx` (+101/−7): toggle `<button aria-pressed aria-label>`
  and a native `<select id="metronome-count-in" name …>` with an sr-only
  `<label htmlFor>`, placed at the right edge of the footer's left grid track
  — **not on the transport plate**, whose 2 + PLAY + 2 key count is what
  holds PLAY on the viewport centre. PLAY's label reads "Counting in - press
  to cancel" during a count. Tailwind sweep on the whole file: zero hits.
- Builder verification: tsc clean; `metronome.test.ts` ok (written first;
  one real red on `4019.9999999999995` vs `4020`; accent assertion
  mutation-checked); `npm test` 111/0.
- Follow-ups it left: `cancelMetronomeCountIn()` exported but not wired to
  START/END; count-in→transport hand-off can lag on a cold first play
  (`liveMixer.start()` is async and exposes no "start at ctx time"); no
