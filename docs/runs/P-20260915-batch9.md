## Design decisions (lead)

- T19 was split at dispatch: its offline mirror became T19b, run after
  T18, because two wave-1 tickets would otherwise have shared
  `renderCore.ts`. Two workers never share a file; the split cost one
  extra review cycle and no rework.
- Pan/in-chain lead = the track's OWN insert-chain latency (T19's
  deviation), not the whole path to the sum: the panner precedes the
  comp and every downstream bus. Offline the same rule, over the chain
  actually built, gated on `includeFx`.
- Undo coalescing keys are derived at the CONTROL level (T23's
  deviation): an armed ride writes the track and the lane every frame,
  so per-target keys would have made one step per frame. Anonymous
  writes share one null identity (pinned by the raw-`setState` and
  `placeTakes` cases); `{ coalesce: true }` stays a join-anything
  override (pinned by the bus-fader + send-knob gesture). A hold frame
  joins only a NAMED open step.
- An armed EMPTY track does not capture MIDI (T22): the mic engine
  already lands a take on every armed track. The mic-side skip for
  instrument tracks is T26's (wave 2a), with `capturesMidi` the one
  definition both sides import.
- NodeF.I.'s audio-rate branch stays on its own wiring (T20): a mod
  port takes an `AudioNode` and the lfo node's oscillator is shared and
  `t0`-locked. A node-port `ModTarget` variant is a follow-up.
- The comp `DelayNode` carries `channelCount = 2` +
  `channelCountMode = 'explicit'` offline AND live: T18 measured the
  tail loss through a panner, so the pin is the guarantee.
- The offline render pads the context by the declared-latency bound
  for the MASTER scope only; stems/selections keep T14's truncation,
  pinned, pending a call on the harness's case-C reference.
