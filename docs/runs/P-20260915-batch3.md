## T04 — what changed

- `lib/decodeCache.ts`: `decodeClipBlob(ctx, blob)` / `peekDecoded(ctx, blob)`;
  a `WeakMap<Blob, Map<sampleRate, AudioBuffer>>` plus the same shape for
  in-flight promises. Only a successful decode is cached; the in-flight slot
  is released by identity on either settlement; the 15 s timeout timer is
  cleared once the race is decided (the four old copies leaked it).
- **Ticket premise corrected by the builder:** the plan said the renderers
  decode at the device rate. They don't — all three pin
  `new AudioContext({ sampleRate: 44100 })`; the engine context is created at
  the device rate (`playerStore.ts:86`). Sharing one buffer blind would
  resample 44.1→48→44.1 and make a bounce's bytes depend on whether play had
  been pressed first. Hence the **rate key**. Lead decision: accept.
  Consequence, stated exactly: 44.1 kHz device → full live↔bounce sharing,
  peak 2N→N; 48 kHz device → live↔bounce does not share, peak unchanged at
  2N, steady-state rises by one 44.1 kHz buffer per clip (renderer buffers
  now retained for the Blob's lifetime). Bounce↔bounce dedupe (freeze eight
  tracks = decode once) applies everywhere. Product follow-up recorded in
  the plan §3.13: run the renderers at the engine rate, or resample at
  export.
- Converted: `liveMixer` (all three readers + `ensureDecoded`), the three
  offline renderers, `playSelectedPreview`, and (rework) `extractRegionWav`.
  Left alone: `cropAudioBlob` (arbitrary `Blob` parameter), `StepSequencer`
  (no clip decode), `DawSessionGrid` (URL-keyed HTTP source).
- Accepted behaviour change: the preview and `extractRegionWav` now inherit
  the shared 15 s timeout they never had.

## T05 — what changed

- `lib/tempoMap.ts`: pure functions over a sorted `TempoEvent[]`
  (`beat`, `bpm`, optional authoritative `timeSec`): `normalizeTempoMap`,
  `getTempoAtBeat`, `getSecPerBeatAt`, `beatToTime`, `timeToBeat`, and
  meter-aware `getBeatAtBar` / `getBarAtBeat` delegating to `meterMap.ts`.
  Piecewise-constant; ramps documented as not implemented. Empty/invalid map
  → seeded 120 bpm event (Tracktion's never-empty rule). Written from the
  mathematical spec; header cites ACE and Tracktion as design-only with
  licences named.
- `lib/beatClock.ts`: every export kept; `phase`/`nextGrid`/`timeOf`/
  `setAnchor`/`setBpm` compute through the tempo module; `beatsPerBar` is a
  `meterAtBar` lookup; added `setMeterMap`, `meterMap`, `beatsPerBarAt`,
  optional `bar` on `gridSec`/`barSec`. **81 values captured before the
  rewire and diffed after: bit-for-bit identical**, now pinned in
  `beatClock.test.ts`. `nextGrid('bar')` correct in 7/8 and across a meter
  change.
- `lib/notechart.ts`: `secondsFromBeats`/`beatsFromSeconds` are thin wrappers
  over the module; a `WeakMap` caches the `TempoEntry[]→TempoEvent[]` shape.
  One deliberate, asserted ≤1 ULP divergence (`60/bpm` form vs `(x*60)/bpm`)
  for tempos whose `60/bpm` is inexact; all consumers compare at 1e-9.

### Lead finding: performance regression, fixed

Lead micro-benchmark (20 000 conversions, tsx) of the first version against
the replaced notechart algorithm: N=1 → 5×, N=50 → 51×, **N=500 → 519×**
(640 ms vs 1.2 ms) — `beatToTime`/`timeToBeat` re-normalized (Map + sort +
allocate) per call and scanned linearly. Rework: normalization memoized by
input-array identity (`WeakMap`), frozen shared default, binary search.
Builder: N=500 → 1.2×; **lead re-run: 2.8 ms vs 1.8 ms (≈1.5×), was 640 ms.**
New obligation, documented in three places and pinned by a test: a
`TempoEvent[]` is immutable once handed in — change tempo by passing a new
array (both current callers already do).
